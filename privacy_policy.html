Introduction

Welcome to Family Tree Forever ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your family's information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our Service:

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Phone number (optional)

Family Tree Data

  • Names of family members
  • Birth dates and places
  • Death dates and places (if applicable)
  • Biographical information
  • Relationships
  • Custom fields you create

Media Content

  • Photos you upload
  • Videos you upload
  • Voice recordings
  • Documents and scanned images

User-Generated Content

  • Memories and stories
  • Comments and notes
  • Time capsules
  • Digital wills
  • Tags and annotations

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect:

Device Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information
  • IP address

Usage Information

  • Pages viewed
  • Features used
  • Time spent on Service
  • Search queries
  • Click patterns
  • Error logs

Location Information (Optional)

  • GPS coordinates (only when you tag memories with location)
  • Location metadata from photos
  • You can disable location services at any time

5. Your Privacy Rights

5.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you're in the EU, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Export your data
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw Consent: At any time

5.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you're in California, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect
  • Know if we sell your data (we don't)
  • Delete your data
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

5.3 Account Deletion (Global Right)

We provide two easy ways for you to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant):
    • Go to Settings → Account → Tap Delete Account
    • You will be asked to confirm your choice
    • Once confirmed, your account and all associated data (family trees, memories, personal info) will be permanently deleted from our servers
  2. Web Request (No App Required):
    • If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, you can request deletion via our Account Deletion Web Form
    • Upon receiving your request, we will verify your identity via email and process the deletion within 30 days

Important: Deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Active Accounts

We retain your data while your account is active:

  • Family tree data: Indefinitely
  • Photos and media: Indefinitely
  • Account information: Until you delete account
  • Usage logs: 90 days

6.2 Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:

  • Personal data: Deleted within 30 days
  • Backups: Purged within 90 days
  • Legal holds: Retained as required by law

7. Children's Privacy

7.1 Age Requirements

  • You must be 13+ to create an account
  • Children under 13 can be added to trees
  • Parents/guardians control children's data

7.2 COPPA Compliance (US)

For children under 13:

  • We collect only necessary information
  • Parents can review and delete data
  • No targeted advertising to children
